🚀 System Overview General

How ApexBoard Roles Work

All Users

ApexBoard automatically tailors its interface based on your logged-in role:

  • Team Member: Focused view with 7 key tabs (My Overview, Onboarding Hub, Message Board, Instruction Videos, Projects, Reports, Leaderboard) plus personal tasks and work-time logger.
  • Manager: Operational oversight with full Projects Directory, Onboarding progress tracker, Team roster, Work Reports, and Leaderboard.
  • Administrator: Global authority with Team Directory modification rights, Member Deactivation & Deletion, Recovery Vault, Audit Logs, and Project configuration.

👤 Team Member How-To Guides For Members

How to Use My Overview & Track Your XP

Member

Your personal command center displays your current Level, total earned Experience Points (XP), assigned enterprise projects, and active tasks.

  • Click My Overview in the sidebar.
  • View your Level and XP Progress Bar at the top to see how close you are to your next level rank.
  • Check the Assigned Projects Hub to view cards for every project your admin has linked to you.
  • Click directly on any project card to open its task spreadsheet (MondayTable) or Kanban board.
  • If you have pending project requests, view the pending project notification card to know your access state.

How to Upload a Custom Profile Photo or Choose an Avatar

Member

Personalize your identity across the leaderboard, task assignments, and message board:

  • Navigate to My Overview.
  • Hover over your profile avatar badge and click the Upload Photo / Avatar icon or button.
  • Option A — Upload Device Photo: Click the file upload area, select any JPEG/PNG/WebP image from your computer, preview it, and hit Save Profile Photo.
  • Option B — Pick Stylized Avatar: Choose one of the illustrated avatar presets (Tech, Executive, Creative, Adventurer) for instant synchronization.
  • Your new avatar updates immediately across your top header, sidebar, and audit history.

How to Complete the Onboarding Wizard & Claim +5 XP

Member

New team members can quickly ramp up and earn bonus XP during onboarding:

  • Open Onboarding Panel from the sidebar.
  • Follow the 3-step interactive onboarding flow:
    • Step 1 (Welcome & Identity): Verify or update your full name. Clicking continue grants an immediate +5 XP celebration bonus!
    • Step 2 (Readiness Checklist): Complete foundational checkboxes (Review SOPs, Join Message Board, Connect with Team Lead) to gain progress.
    • Step 3 (Graduation): Review your completed badges and graduate to active status.
  • Your onboarding progress automatically reflects in the manager's talent oversight dashboard.

How to Manage Tasks in Monday Table & Kanban Board

Member

ApexBoard offers dual visualization modes for your daily work:

  • Select any project under Assigned Boards in your sidebar.
  • Switch between Table (structured spreadsheet) and Kanban (visual columns) using the view toggles at the top right.
  • Click any task title to open the Task Modal to view descriptions, attachments, and discussion logs.
  • To update task status, change the dropdown (Not Started, In Progress, In Review, Completed) or drag-and-drop the card to the next Kanban column.
  • When you mark an assigned task as Completed, your earned XP reward is automatically banked into your total!

How to Log Work Time & Productivity Hours

Member

Keep your team and manager updated on the hours invested in each milestone:

  • Click the green "Log Work Time" button located at the bottom of the sidebar or in the top navigation bar.
  • Select the Project you worked on from the dropdown list.
  • Choose the specific Task or type a work summary if it was general maintenance.
  • Input the Duration (in hours or decimal, e.g. 2.5) and date.
  • Write a brief description of what was accomplished and click Submit Work Log.
  • Your submission immediately logs into the central Reports hub for managerial payroll and productivity tracking.

How to Watch Instruction Videos & SOPs

Member

Access internal standard operating procedures and video training material:

  • Click Instruction Videos (SOP) in the sidebar.
  • Browse the video library: videos assigned to "All Members" or specifically targeted to your account will be visible.
  • Click on any video card to play directly in the embedded video player.
  • Mark completed videos to help your manager track your technical training achievements.

How to Use the Team Message Board

Member

Communicate asynchronously with your teammates and read important organization announcements:

  • Select Message Board (HQ) in the sidebar.
  • Review pinned announcements and important system alerts from administrators at the top of the feed.
  • Type a new message or update in the composer at the top, select priority tags if needed, and hit Post Message.
  • Reply to teammates' messages by clicking Reply to keep discussions organized.

🛡️ Admin & Manager How-To Guides For Admins

How to Monitor the Admin Overview Dashboard

Admin & Manager

The executive dashboard provides high-level transparency into all operations:

  • Click Admin Overview (or Management Overview) in the sidebar.
  • Review KPI Cards: Total Active Projects, Open Tasks, Total Team Size, and Hours Logged this cycle.
  • Examine the Recent Activity Stream to see who created tasks, updated statuses, or completed onboarding.
  • Click any quick link in the right rail to jump immediately into project creation or team audit logs.

How to Create and Manage Enterprise Projects

Admin & Manager

Projects form the foundation of workflows, assigning teams, tasks, and budgets:

  • Navigate to Projects in the sidebar.
  • Click the + New Project button at the top right.
  • Fill out the project details:
    • Project Name & Key: e.g., "OmniChannel E-Commerce", key "OMNI"
    • Accent Color: Choose a recognizable visual tag
    • Lifecycle Stage: Discovery, In Development, QA/Testing, Production Ready
    • Assign Team Members: Select members who will see this board in their sidebar
  • Click Create Project. The project immediately appears in both the directory and assigned members' navigation.
  • To edit an existing project, click the ⋯ (More Actions) menu on its card and select Edit Project.

How to Create Tasks & Assign XP Rewards

Admin

Incentivize top performance by configuring tasks with automated XP rewards:

  • Open any project board, or click the + Add Task button on the task table.
  • Enter the Task Title and detailed requirements in the description editor.
  • Assign one or more Team Members from the directory dropdown.
  • Set the Priority Level (Low, Medium, High, Urgent) and Due Date.
  • Specify the Completion XP Reward (e.g. 50 XP, 100 XP, 250 XP).
  • Click Save Task. When the assignee finishes the task, their user level automatically levels up!

How to Add, Deactivate, or Delete Team Members

Admin Exclusive

Admins have full Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) governance over team accounts:

  • Click Team Directory in the sidebar (Managers have read-only view; Admins have write permissions).
  • Adding a Member: Click + Add Member, enter their Full Name, Email, Temporary Password, Department, and Designation.
  • Deactivating a Member: Click the Deactivate button on any active member card. Their status changes to inactive, and their record is safely moved to the Recovery Vault to prevent accidental data loss.
  • Permanent Deletion: If a member record was created by error, click Delete and confirm the security prompt.
⚠️
Security Rule: Managers can view the directory but are prevented by RBAC server guards from deleting or deactivating members.

How to Restore Members from the Recovery Vault

Admin Exclusive

Never worry about accidental deactivations with the built-in soft-delete safety vault:

  • Click Recovery Vault in the sidebar (displays the number of deactivated members in an amber badge).
  • Review the list of deactivated accounts, showing original role, deactivation date, and email.
  • Click Restore Member on the respective account.
  • The member account is instantly reactivated, restoring their project access, task history, and XP leaderboard position without data loss.

How to Broadcast Urgent Organization Notices

Admin

Broadcast mission-critical updates that require immediate acknowledgement by all staff:

  • In the top navbar or sidebar, click the Broadcast Notice or Megaphone icon.
  • Enter the Notice Headline, Notice Body text, and Urgency Level (Normal, High Priority, Urgent/Critical).
  • Click Publish Notice.
  • The modal or banner displays automatically for all signed-in users on their next action, ensuring zero missed communications.

How to Switch Languages or Upload Custom Language Packs

Admin & Manager

ApexBoard supports international teams with modular JSON translation dictionaries:

  • Locate the Language selector at the bottom of the sidebar.
  • To switch between built-in languages, select English or Svenska (Swedish) from the dropdown. All UI strings adapt in real time.
  • Uploading a Custom Language JSON: Admins can click Upload Custom JSON to upload a localized dictionary (e.g. German, French, Spanish). The app validates the keys and activates the new language instantly!

How to Deploy Live Updates in 1 Click

DevOps & Admin

ApexBoard includes a fully automated deployment pipeline. When you are ready to publish code or database updates live:

npm run deploy

What happens under the hood:

  • Automatically commits changes and pushes to GitHub repository main branch.
  • Builds the optimized production client bundle with Vite.
  • Publishes directly to live Netlify CDN (https://bizproject-80307.netlify.app).
  • Performs automatic live health check on Render backend (https://bizproject-api.onrender.com).
